AI-powered student analytics · May 2026

Identifying student disengagement before it becomes dropout risk

Business problem
Identify early signs of student disengagement and potential dropout risk using academic performance and online learning behaviour.
Method
Analysed 26K+ students, 10M+ LMS engagement records, and 170K+ assessment records, developing machine learning models using Python and Scikit-learn.
Finding
Gradient Boosting achieved 81.83% test accuracy, with 81.69% cross-validation accuracy, identifying students across 3 risk categories.
Recommendation
Use risk predictions and engagement patterns to help educators identify students who may
need early academic support and intervention.

Retail business intelligence · January 2026

Making a large retail dataset useful for decisions

Business problem
Understand sales performance and customer behaviour across a large retail dataset.
Method
Cleaned, analysed, and validated 400K+ rows with Python and SQL, then built Power BI views with forecasting and churn analysis.
Finding
The analysis surfaced £8.83M in revenue alongside patterns in customer behaviour and sales trends.
Recommendation
Use the dashboard to investigate churn and sales patterns by customer and period before prioritising retention or growth actions.

Sales performance · January 2025

Giving sales and profit more than one point of view

Business problem
Make revenue and profit performance easier to monitor and investigate.
Method
Built a Power BI dashboard with several chart types, interactive slicers, Q&A, key influencers, and a decomposition tree.
Finding
The interactive views give users ways to examine performance and explore contributing factors; the CV does not report specific quantified findings.
Recommendation
Use the exploration features to follow up on changes in revenue and profit, then validate possible drivers against the underlying business context.
